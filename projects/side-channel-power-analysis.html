Side-Channel Power Analysis Pipeline

As an undergraduate researcher at the University of Delaware, I implemented an open-source pipeline covering RTL-to-GDSII chip design, gate-level netlist activity simulation, dynamic power tracing, and side-channel analysis of cryptographic hardware.

My approach

  • Built the flow with open-source tools: Yosys for synthesis, OpenROAD for place-and-route, and Icarus Verilog for simulation.
  • Optimized Python scripts that simulate dynamic power consumption, improving Pearson correlation coefficients in later correlation power analysis.
  • Extended the analysis beyond gate level with layout-level data for more accurate power simulation.

Results

  • Ran the open-source flow in 16 hours, compared with 24 hours for commercial flows.
  • Produced more accurate power simulations and side-channel evaluations than the commercial gate-level flow.
