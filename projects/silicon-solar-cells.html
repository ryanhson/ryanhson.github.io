Silicon Solar Cell Fabrication

I fabricated silicon solar cells from start to finish (wafer preparation, diffusion, HF etching, and metal contact deposition), then characterized the finished devices.

The problem

Placeholder: what problem were you solving, and why did it matter?

My approach

  • Prepared wafers, performed diffusion, and HF-etched the surface.
  • Deposited Al and Ti/Pd/Ag thin-film contacts with electron-beam physical vapor deposition, and analyzed deposition rate, thickness, and beam-current data.
  • Characterized devices with four-point probe measurements, illuminated I-V testing, and quantum efficiency measurements from 340–1200 nm.

Results

  • Achieved efficiencies up to 7.3%.
  • Achieved corrected quantum efficiency up to 77%.
